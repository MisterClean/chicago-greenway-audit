# Dearborn, Sunnyside and Rosehill source review

Reviewed October 27, 2026. [Structured evidence](../data/routes/review-evidence.json) covers the five exact Neighborhood Greenway city rows. The city inventory is a designation source; the local OSM snapshot is effective September 11, 2026. All movement conclusions are source-inferred and need current sign and field verification. Stops, signals, speed humps and advisory lanes are not themselves continuity breaks.

Dearborn Goethe–Schiller and Schiller–North are separate city rows sharing OSM way 31009529; Schiller is node 261171337, Burton is node 261171338. The way runs north with no one-way tag. The [June 13, 2024 alderman letter](https://x.com/streetsblogchi/status/1812173108645691557), whose first-page image was visually inspected, describes two-way motor traffic and requests removal of advisory markings from Division to North. This is historical evidence, not confirmation of current signs or the outcome of the request.

Sunnyside Maplewood–Western comprises ways 1124079045, 1124079047, 1124079048 and 117007063, all without one-way tags. Western–Lincoln is way 24165992 in eastbound order, tagged one-way with a bicycle exception. The city likewise records eastbound motor direction; the westbound bicycle lane does not permit westbound cars. The [47th Ward participatory budgeting retrospective](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/5cc380feab1a626e95e25356/t/677efcbdea7fea3e4a12da92/1736375485118/Previous+Participatory+Budgeting+Projects.pdf), PDF page 2, describes installation of the westbound bicycle lane, Oakley curb extensions and Western bicycle signal; the page was rendered and visually inspected. A [2022 project report](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2022/08/10/the-sunnyside-contraflow-lane-a-little-infrastructure-that-makes-a-big-difference) corroborates the eastbound one-way motor segment. Do not assign that rule to the western city row.

Rosehill Ravenswood–Ashland is one city row. The [40th Ward project page](https://40thward.org/project/rosehill-resurfacing-and-bikeway/) identifies it as a connector to Clark's bike lanes under the Ashland resurfacing project and labels it completed; that label does not verify each movement. At Clark, named ways 678895681 and 24093741 do not directly meet: one-way circular residential way 1535609224 links their endpoint nodes 10943460512 and 13990554011. Both directions require their respective directed arcs around this mapped circle; the detailed node order is in the JSON. At Ravenswood, relation 9454355 prohibits the right turn from Ravenswood way 681310579 via node 257172600 to Rosehill way 678895681. This is a specific approach restriction, not a Rosehill through-route restriction. The local exhaustive scan of restriction relations touching all reviewed way nodes found that relation and a Western no-U-turn relation 15034040; absence of a mapped restriction does not establish current signs.

No detailed block-level CDOT drawing or as-built was located for the exact scopes. Searches are documented in the JSON. The ward retrospective is not a design drawing; proposed or project-wide completion descriptions do not establish current access.

The observations and search metadata are preserved in research batch `third-ten-central-three-2026-10-27` of the authoritative evidence file.

# Winnemac and Ainslie source review

Reviewed 2026-10-27 for an ordinary passenger car making a through trip. These are geographically scoped source inferences, not field approvals. A bicycle contraflow lane is not permission for cars to travel against a one-way. Traffic signals, speed humps and a traversable traffic circle do not themselves end a through trip; a designated bend is followed where a legal connection is verified.

## Winnemac

- The [saved city bicycle routes snapshot](https://data.cityofchicago.org/resource/hvv9-38ut.json), retrieved 2026-09-19, describes Rockwell–Leavitt as eastbound for cars with two-way bicycles and contraflow. It does not prove the current signs or every Lincoln or Western crossing. The city designation is discontinuous from Leavitt to Damen across Winnemac Park; do not infer a car connection across the park from the two named segments.
- CDOT's [Winnemac Neighborhood Greenway flyer](https://api.chicago.gov/filenet5/servlets/getDocumentContent?applicationId=CompleteStreets&documentId=%7B3002F197-0000-C61B-818B-608D8AAF30D2%7D), PDF pages 1–2, proposes a Damen–Broadway greenway and states Clark–Broadway would become one-way for vehicles. The page 1 map shows east-pointing one-way-except-bikes arrows. The city snapshot likewise reports eastbound motor direction and contraflow on Clark–Glenwood and Glenwood–Broadway. These support the eastbound design and snapshot, not a current field verification. The flyer anticipates construction in summer 2025, not completion; page 2 explains generic treatments, not specific intersection geometry. SHA-256 `49896d442c886900b282e6918ebca17b1b7f61feea3e9c6c5b96ffd32cdab911`; PDF creation metadata 2025-05-27, not a confirmed publication date.
- At Glenwood the city explicitly includes the short northward connector `row-muht_4bn5-v2q2`, linking the offset east-west rows. Its motor direction is unspecified. The flyer is a small-scale schematic and does not show signs, legal turns, or intersection-scale geometry for this jog. The motor connection must be checked; an apparent matching gap is not evidence of a barrier. On Damen–Clark the city rows contain no cardinal one-way direction; missing data is not proof that all crossings work in both directions. The flyer's advisory bike-lane example and speed humps do not establish a diverter.
- CDOT's [Lincoln Avenue Streetscape public meeting boards](https://40thward.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/Lincoln_Public-Meeting-4-Boards_red.pdf), PDF page 3, depict removal of a dedicated Lincoln left-turn lane at Winnemac while left turns remain permitted. The [40th Ward update](https://40thward.org/2026/02/lincoln-avenue-north-streetscape-update/) reports the northbound dedicated left-turn lane was removed in 2025. This does not establish every Winnemac movement. The board is a design presentation, not an as-built drawing. ## Ainslie

- The city snapshot's Rockwell–Lincoln row reports eastbound motor traffic and two-way bicycles with contraflow. Western–Damen has no cardinal motor one-way value; it is insufficient alone to establish two-way travel or crossing permissions. These are separated designated components, and the connection between them must be separately established. The [47th Ward 2023 participatory-budget preview](https://pbstanford.org/chicago47_2023_young/approval) proposed traffic calming on Western–Damen, mentioning curb extensions among possible measures. This was a ballot preview, not evidence of selection, a built diverter, or an interruption of car travel.
- The CDOT Lincoln boards page 3 show the proposed prohibition of the **westbound Ainslie to southbound Western left turn**. The 40th Ward's [February 19, 2026 update](https://40thward.org/2026/02/lincoln-avenue-north-streetscape-update/) announced the change and said the eastbound-to-northbound left turn would remain. A [March 20 ward newsletter](https://chicago-newsletter-aggregator.open-advocacy.com/newsletter/871554d2-5be3-4d4a-922d-548da0428ca8) said implementation was delayed pending signal inspection and programming; a [June 26 newsletter](https://chicago-newsletter-aggregator.open-advocacy.com/newsletter/a54e65bd-9be1-4207-9143-888c70afe0d4) reports functional lights and no-left-turn signs added. These newsletters are ward communications reproduced by an aggregator. Verify the current sign and signal; this left-turn restriction is not evidence that a car cannot continue straight on Ainslie.

## Search and limitations

Searches covered CDOT/Complete Streets and 40th and 47th Ward sites for each corridor, project names, detailed PDFs, traffic circles, diverters and one-way changes. The 40th Ward's [2024 infrastructure update](https://40thward.org/2024/06/2024-development-and-infrastructure-improvements-coming-to-the-40th-ward/) announced new neighborhood bikeways on both streets; it did not specify individual motor movements. The CDOT flyer and Lincoln public meeting boards were retrieved, text extracted, and relevant complete pages visually rendered and inspected: Winnemac flyer pages 1–2 and Lincoln boards page 3. Neither is a full intersection-by-intersection greenway engineering plan or signed as-built. No detailed Ainslie greenway plan, Winnemac Glenwood-jog drawing, or corridor-wide as-built was located in these searches. No source examined establishes a car-blocking traffic circle or diverter on either designated corridor; absence of a discovered source is not proof none exists. Inspect current signs and each crossing, including the Glenwood jog, and obtain final detailed plans and installation records before assigning verified movement overrides.

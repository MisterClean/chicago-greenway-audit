# Carmen and Argyle source review

Reviewed 2026-10-27. These are scoped source inferences, not verified motor movements or whole-route approvals. The designated route can bend at connected street segments; stops, signals and speed humps do not by themselves prevent a through trip. A bicycle trail or an off-route detour is not a car connection.

## Carmen

The saved [Chicago Bike Routes inventory](https://data.cityofchicago.org/resource/hvv9-38ut.json), whose rows were last updated 2025-12-16 and saved 2026-09-19, has four Carmen rows: Clark–Glenwood (`row-567r_3xw8.pymc`), Glenwood–Broadway (`row-ttku-y8f5_da8e`), Spaulding–Kedzie (`row-nfrj.vcdz_ctwq`), and Kedzie–Albany (`row-rfjh_buwp~qfix`). The first three have no cardinal motor direction; a blank does not establish two-way driving. For Kedzie–Albany the city says eastbound. The local OSM snapshot dated 2026-09-11 has eastbound `oneway=yes` on Albany-side way [583422340](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/583422340), but its adjacent Kedzie-side [1257543990](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1257543990) has no one-way tag. This discrepancy needs current signs and the original traffic order. City fields and OSM each have spatial and temporal limits; neither establishes junction permissions.

[Streetsblog's 2017 account](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2017/05/03/it-wont-be-long-until-the-glenwood-greenway-is-ready-to-ride) describes CDOT's proposed Carmen/Glenwood–Broadway shared markings and a planned refuge island at Carmen/Broadway. It is historical secondary reporting of plans, not an as-built drawing; it does not determine car turns. Its proposed refuge is not evidence of a complete roadway closure. [A 2026 field report](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2026/10/21/sbc-bike-lane-fest-2025-26-far-northwest-side-including-albany-park-forest-glen-and-north-park) describes the bicycle route by Spaulding connecting to a riverside trail. The trail is not evidence of a permitted motor route.

The [47th Ward newsletter of July 2, 2021](https://myemail.constantcontact.com/47th-Ward-Newsletter---July-2--2021.html?aid=XdZUkjmDc8g&soid=1132690428543) discusses restoration on the 1400 block and a prior temporary Carmen/Glenwood sewer closure; it does not establish a permanent restriction. The [September 22, 2025 council committee summary](https://occprodstoragev1.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/meetingattachmentspublic/218e4fd6-b343-4672-b6c1-c5e1abacb1e1.pdf), PDF and printed p. 7, lists a separate short Carmen one-way westbound except bicycles, from 5051 N Spaulding to the alley at approximately 3241 W Carmen. The [signed 39th Ward memorandum](https://occprodstoragev1.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/matterattachmentspublic/3f2a3e0a-9296-48b5-b87c-af643be10ab3.pdf), dated September 11, 2025, page 1, uses the same wording; the scanned page was visually inspected (SHA-256 `1294286db1e1331f24248e39bcf66971291359bf8747726ae575ebe864a6be70`). In the saved OSM geometry, westbound Carmen [way 1081688366](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1081688366) extends from Spaulding northeast to node 2142142005 at -87.7104738, 41.9739068, where [alley 204193953](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/204193953) and eastward Carmen [way 24076762](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/24076762) meet. The city Spaulding–Kedzie line starts about 4 m east of that node, at -87.7104319, 41.9738908. This supports an inference that the named spur lies west of and meets the designated line, but the wording gives an approximate address, not surveyed coordinates. Exact overlap and endpoint turns remain unresolved; do not extend the rule to the whole row. [Councilmatic](https://chicago.councilmatic.org/legislation/o2025-0019367/) reports the 39th Ward-sponsored item passed September 25, 2025; this is not verification of current signs.

## Argyle

Four saved city rows describe the disconnected Argyle pieces: river–Francisco (`row-mfd8-e3sw-kr45`), Francisco–Rockwell (`row-nn2f.26nq.pskp`), Pulaski–Ridgeway (`row-izhx-npfm~7itr`), and Kedvale–Pulaski (`row-wkph~qm3w~5yab`). The city gives westbound motor direction on Francisco–Rockwell and Kedvale–Pulaski, and no direction on the other two. The saved OSM ways [24078652](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/24078652) and [1443715350](https://www.openstreetmap.org/way/1443715350), respectively, run west with `oneway=yes` and a bicycle exception. An eastbound bicycle exception does not permit eastbound cars. The first way spans Francisco–Rockwell; the second spans Pulaski–Kedvale. Their node order was checked in the saved OSM snapshot, not surveyed in the field.

The ward-branded [Argyle proposal map](https://chi.streetsblog.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2024/08/GUTdHoOW4AAUuT2.jpeg), reproduced by Streetsblog and credited to CDOT, was visually inspected: it compares the then-existing two-way bridge with a proposed westbound arrow extending toward Whipple. The map is conceptual, not an as-built engineering drawing. Image SHA-256: `3ec4b038efe767416dc3435a13c0d76dfe633d42657f63fe2448a4c051ff8722`.

A [2024 report](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2024/08/16/cdot-alders-rodriguez-and-vasquez-plan-to-make-argyle-at-north-shore-channel-trail-one-way-wb) attributes a westbound conversion proposal at the Argyle bridge to CDOT and the 33rd and 40th Wards. It describes the bridge then as two-way and says a community meeting would inform design. It is secondary reporting, and the proposal is not proof that a conversion was approved or installed. The 40th Ward's [October 15, 2026 People's Budget update](https://40thward.org/2026/10/announcing-the-2027-peoples-budget/) places flashing pedestrian beacons on Argyle between California and the river on a ballot. That is a proposal, not a funded or completed project and does not resolve the bridge direction or motor turns. The local snapshot's lack of a one-way tag on bridge approach ways is not proof of current two-way access.

The September 2025 [committee summary](https://occprodstoragev1.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/meetingattachmentspublic/218e4fd6-b343-4672-b6c1-c5e1abacb1e1.pdf), PDF and printed p. 7, specifies Argyle Kedvale–Pulaski westbound except bicycles and a 20 mph limit Kedvale–Ridgeway. [Councilmatic](https://chicago.councilmatic.org/legislation/o2025-0019367/) reports the 39th Ward-sponsored direction item passed council. This corroborates the recorded direction without confirming current installation. A speed limit alone does not interrupt continuity. The PDF page was visually rendered and reviewed; SHA-256 `e0b48bd4dc5f14f44b8ab16c0faf29c4d8363e9bb3315c2b61bdcadab0fd77b2`.

## Source gaps and next checks

Searches for Carmen/Glenwood/Broadway CDOT plans, Argyle/Francisco/Whipple conversion, and Argyle/Kedvale/Pulaski projects, including ward sites and Chicago's project pages, did not locate original intersection-scale CDOT design sheets, a signed as-built set, or a direct ward conversion decision. A linked [CDOT Mid Northeast project page](https://www.chicago.gov/city/en/sites/complete-streets-chicago/home/featured-projects-and-innovations/projects0/MidNortheastNeighborhoodBikeNetwork.html) returned HTTP 403 to the research tool. No detailed engineering PDF or signed as-built was obtained. The committee summary PDF and the reproduced ward-branded proposal image were reviewed as described above. Targeted searches did not identify a direct 33rd Ward meeting record, 39th Ward project notice, or original 48th Ward Carmen design sheet. The report’s linked larger Flickr copy was unavailable to the web tool. Record-level search attempts and missing artifacts are in the `data/routes/review-evidence.json`.

Request the original plans, traffic orders and completion records from CDOT and the relevant wards, then inspect current regulatory signs, direction at both ends, intermediate turns, island geometry and any barriers. Distinguish design, construction and field conditions. No circle or diverter classification is supported by the sources located here; lack of a drawing is not proof of absence.

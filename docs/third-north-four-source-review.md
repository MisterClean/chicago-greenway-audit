# Albion, Birchwood, Eastlake and Jonquil source review

Reviewed October 27, 2026. [Structured evidence](../data/routes/review-evidence.json) covers all seven exact city Neighborhood Greenway rows. These are connected route components, not necessarily seven independent projects. The city inventory's blank direction does not assert two-way motor access. The September 11, 2026 OSM snapshot is evidence of mapped geometry and tags, not verification of present signs.

Albion Glenwood–Lakewood is mapped eastbound. At the elevated railway, two residential arms (458591863 and 1178008329) share the same endpoints and both point east; they are not opposing carriageways. Both attach to the same mapped alley at separate interior nodes. At approximately 1 m sampling against the city row, their maximum offsets are about 2.5 m and 6.7 m respectively; the former is closer but it is not established that only it belongs to the designation. Both are tagged motor residential ways, not service or bicycle-only. Both have the same source-inferred eastbound through access, with no westbound through access, and share approaches and exits. Each has an attachment to the same alley at a distinct interior node, so off-route movements should not be treated as identical. All 5,723 staged restriction relations were scanned for the arms, nodes and incident ways, with no matches. The shorter arm can represent one mapped car path; this does not identify the sole designated lane or verify present signs. East of Lakewood, OSM 458591864 remains one-way east to node 2174877400, then 1191237293 is two-way to Sheridan. Sheridan–Winthrop way 24103754 has no oneway tag. City blank values make no counterassertion; a bicycle contraflow lane does not permit westbound cars.

Birchwood Ashland–Sheridan is eastbound in the city and OSM. A mapped one-way circular residential way 1535604025 bridges the gap between 1535604024 and 104177858: enter node 13990513312 and proceed along the circular way's ordered nodes, wrapping through 13990513335 to exit 13990513311. East of Sheridan, way 104177859 lacks a one-way tag. Eastlake's 24114915 and 34073688 are one-way south in node order, matching the city S. The [May 1, 2024 City Council committee agenda](https://occprodstoragev1.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/meetingattachmentspublic/b295bd5e-b31c-4fc0-bfe4-d7e5ed40f2e8.pdf) lists direct introductions for Birchwood eastbound except bicycles and Eastlake southbound except bicycles, plus speed limits; this agenda alone does not confirm enactment or installed signs. A [2026 Streetsblog photo report](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2026/09/30/sbc-bike-lane-fest-2025-26-far-north-side-including-lincoln-square-west-ridge-rogers-park-edgewater-and-uptown) identifies greenways at Birchwood/Sheridan and Eastlake/Sheridan. The complete PDF pages 2 and 3 were rendered and visually inspected; their SHA-256 is recorded in the structured evidence.

Jonquil Paulina–Greenview follows 210205666, which has no oneway tag and a 20 mph tag. The [2015 project account](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2015/06/29/roger-that-low-stress-north-south-bike-route-planned-for-rogers-park) describes a draft Greenview/Glenwood route to Howard or Jonquil, not proof of current access. Stop signs, signals, humps and speed limits alone do not break through access.

The staged restriction-relation scan found no matching relation for the named candidates, which does not establish current turning rules. No detailed CDOT block engineering drawing or direct ward construction update for these exact segments was located in the documented searches. Unavailable PDFs and references are leads only; current motor signs and the physical circle remain review items.

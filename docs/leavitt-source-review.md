# Leavitt source review — 2026-10-27

The 12 saved city rows describe two geographically separate greenways: Milwaukee–Lyndale in Bucktown and Diversey–Berwyn north of the river. A Leavitt greenway connection between Lyndale and Diversey is not established by these records. The individual observations in `data/routes/review-evidence.json` bind each city's original row ID. City `oneway_dir` is the motor direction field; `br_oneway`, `br_ow_dir`, and `contraflow` describe bicycles. A two-way bicycle route never gives cars permission to use the contraflow direction. City attributes are coarse snapshot evidence, not a sign survey. Blank motor direction does not prove either two-way movement or unimpeded passage.

The saved city extract labels Milwaukee–Armitage southbound for motor vehicles, with a northbound bicycle exception. It labels Roscoe–Byron, Berteau–Montrose and Wilson–Foster northbound for motor vehicles, with bicycle contraflow. These extents must be checked at the actual block and intersection scale. The 2023 City Council committee report lists proposed bicycle exceptions at Milwaukee–Armitage and Ainslie–Foster; it is not itself proof of current signage. The 40th Ward project page reports Diversey–Berwyn completed in fall 2023 and installed contraflow lanes where Leavitt is one-way, but does not establish current movements at each intersection. Stops, signals and speed humps alone are not motor interruptions.

At Montrose/Lincoln, Leavitt north of Montrose ends in a cul-de-sac. The 47th Ward reported that CDOT largely completed a raised bikeway and curb cuts to connect bicycles through the cul-de-sac; December 2024 on-site reporting corroborates the separated connection. This is evidence of a physical interruption for a car attempting to follow that bicycle connection between the Leavitt legs in either direction. Inspect both approaches, the present barrier and regulatory signs, and any required greenway-following turns; do not infer that a turn is permitted merely because the bicycle route uses it. These reports do not establish a current full-route approval or the status of unrelated intersections.

The ward links CDOT's July 10, 2023 Diversey–Berwyn presentation. The web fetch returned a cache miss, while direct and escalated download attempts could not resolve its host; the linked Complete Streets project page returned 404. The actual drawings were therefore not rendered or visually reviewed, and no conclusion can be drawn from their absence. Searches for a detailed Bucktown plan located 2023 reports and legislative references but no reviewed intersection-scale as-built. The JSON retains the attempted URLs and searches. Current configuration, regulatory signs, and any physical filters beyond the specifically documented cul-de-sac remain field-review gaps.

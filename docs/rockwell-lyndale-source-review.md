# Rockwell and Lyndale source review

Reviewed 2026-10-27 for an ordinary passenger car through trip. The accompanying [research batch](../data/routes/review-evidence.json) binds each observation to exact city row IDs and records source metadata. The September 19 city extract is a snapshot, not a current field survey. Its `oneway_dir` is motor direction; `br_oneway`, `br_ow_dir`, and `contraflow` describe bicycles. A bike exception is not a car exception. Neither a blank motor direction nor an old completion label proves current permission. Stops, signals and humps do not by themselves break a motor route; no physical filter is established here.

## Source search and limits

The 2022 Chicago City Council agenda lists southbound Rockwell Milwaukee–Fullerton and eastbound Lyndale Rockwell–Western, except bicycles. It was inspected as extracted web text, not a visually reviewed design or proof of current signs. The 40th Ward's June 2024 update anticipates a Rockwell bikeway expansion in 2025. A 2026 first ward newsletter, reproduced by the Chicago Alderman Newsletter Tracker, describes a northward Rockwell extension via Altgeld and Schubert. Neither update establishes an individual present-day traffic movement.

Searches combined Rockwell/Lyndale, Fullerton, Lawrence/Argyle/Winnemac, 35th/47th/48th, Brighton Park, CDOT, plans, and ward updates. No detailed corridor-specific CDOT drawing was located for Logan Square Rockwell/Lyndale or South Rockwell, and no project-specific South Rockwell alderman update was found. See the subsequent north-side plan review below; this is a research gap, not evidence that a design does not exist. Search result for the 40th Ward 2025 town hall identifies repaving Lawrence–Ainslie but does not document a physical filter. Seek city project archives and exact plan sheets; verify all junctions in the field before approving movement restrictions.

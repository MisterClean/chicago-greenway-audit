# Granville source review — 2026-10-27

The city snapshot contains ten `GRANVILLE` Neighborhood Greenway rows, Kedzie–Kenmore, including the short Ravenswood–Ravenswood underpass row. The source records in `data/routes/review-evidence.json` preserve every exact city row ID. They describe an ordinary passenger car on a through trip, not field-approved movements. The motor field `oneway_dir` is distinct from bicycle fields `br_oneway`, `br_ow_dir`, and `contraflow`; two-way bicycle access is not permission for a car to go against a one-way.

The city snapshot reports westbound motors on Western–Claremont and on the short Ravenswood underpass, eastbound on Paulina–Clark, westbound on Clark–Greenview, and eastbound on Lakewood–Broadway. The other five rows carry a dash for motor direction; that is not proof that either direction can pass every junction. Opposite one-way blocks are deliberate impediments to an end-to-end car trip, but their presence does not establish a physical diverter. Humps, traffic signals, crossings, and bicycle markings alone are not breaks.

At Ridge and Ravenswood, the [CDOT February 13, 2025 presentation](https://40thward.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Granville-2.13.2025-Presentation.pdf), page 35, shows a west-pointing motor arrow beneath the railway and a separated opposite-direction bicycle lane. The saved OSM ways 1430929462, 1211697096 and 1319363803 likewise have westward node order and `oneway=yes`, with bicycle exceptions. The city’s short `row-hmsb_2f3m-ak9q` identifies the underpass between the two Ravenswood ends; inspect actual approach and turn signs and follow the designated connection, rather than treating an offset or matching gap as an automatic break. The detailed drawing is a proposal, not proof of current signs or all turns.

**Lakewood–Broadway has a documented source conflict.** City `row-cc5q-a6r7~eqj7` reports eastbound and saved OSM ways 815606180 and 1430929469 have eastward node order with `oneway=yes` and `oneway:bicycle=no`. CDOT's presentation page 25 depicts an east-pointing motor arrow west of Broadway; page 26 shows Lakewood. The [traffic-impact schematic](https://40thward.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/Granville-Traffic-Impact-1024x333.png) also shows the arrow toward Broadway and westbound traffic filtered onto Broadway. Yet the [40th Ward project page](https://40thward.org/project/granville-traffic-safety/) and its [June 16, 2025 construction notice](https://40thward.org/2025/06/granville-construction-begins-this-week/) explicitly describe Broadway–Lakewood as westbound. The available design graphic and snapshots support an eastbound source inference, but the conflicting prose must remain visible and current field signs must settle the movement. The presentation identifies east of Clark as the 48th Ward discussion; do not extend the 40th Ward’s own completion report to that section.

The 40th Ward project page lists the project as completed and specifically reports construction completed in its section in August 2025, while its direction list remains future-tense and describes the larger Kedzie–Sheridan project. The June 16 notice anticipated 12 weeks of work and mentioned an extended Clark median, pedestrian islands at Western and Ravenswood, and detached Wolcott bumpouts; it does not establish which current approach movements are blocked. Its [July 3, 2025 advisory-lane update](https://40thward.org/2025/07/new-advisory-bike-lanes-on-granville/) describes installation Fairfield–Clark and shared car use, but cannot override the explicitly one-way segments or verify every junction. The [50th Ward meeting notice](https://50thward.org/event/granville-traffic-safety-meeting/) describes the 50th Ward portion as proposed; no completion is inferred from it.

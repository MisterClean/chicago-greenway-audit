# CDOT neighborhood greenway inventory scope review

Reviewed 2026-10-27. The operational universe is **all rows in CDOT's Bike Routes dataset whose `displayrou` is exactly `Neighborhood Greenway`**, including connector-like or generic labels; it is not a list of named construction projects. The [City's dataset metadata](https://data.cityofchicago.org/api/views/hvv9-38ut.json) identifies the owner as the Department of Transportation, says “Current as of December 2025,” and says updates occur as needed. It describes bike routes but does not define whether each row is built, proposed, retired, or recently modified. All 11 published non-system field descriptions are blank, and there is no installation date, project phase, or planned/completed status field among them. Thus the category is the best available reproducible proxy for CDOT-catalogued greenways, not independent proof of current physical conditions or a guarantee of every 2026 installation.

CDOT's [2014 year-end review](https://chicagocompletestreets.org/wp-content/uploads/2013/06/YearEndReview_2014_Draft_Jan27th_WEB.pdf), page 11, describes neighborhood greenways as typically low-volume residential streets that prioritize walking and biking by reducing speeds and through traffic, and describes Wood as a low-stress connection. The City's [Streets for Cycling Plan 2020](https://www.chicagocompletestreets.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/02/Streets%20for%20Cycling%20Plan%202020.pdf), facilities guide page xi, similarly describes a low-stress residential route using traffic calming or diversion. These are design objectives, not verification of each present segment.

CDOT's [Chicago Cycling Strategy, Spring 2023](https://www.chicago.gov/content/dam/city/depts/cdot/bike/2023/2023_Chicago%20Cycling%20Update.pdf), distinguishes neighborhood greenways from protected bike lanes and trails within the wider low-stress network. The City host returned HTTP 403 during this review; the same CDOT-authored strategy is accessible in [this reproduced text](https://www.scribd.com/document/635776930/Chicago-Cycling-Strategy), especially pages 14, 28–29 and 46–47. [Chicago Complete Streets](https://chicago.gov/completestreets) identifies the strategy as the department's approach. A named-project-only filter is unsupported by the dataset and would exclude catalogued segments; conversely expanding to all low-stress bikeways would change the requested scope. The category is an official classification, not a measured stress rating of every segment.

## Ambiguous names and alternate sources

- `row-3ta3-sx9b.3hwx`: `st_name=AVENUE`, but `street=AVENUE J`, E 100th to E 98th, 0.253279082157 miles. `AVENUE` is a truncated grouping label; preserve the full street and source row.
- `row-v8nm_gcrv.3qmh`: `street=ALLEY`, N Sacramento to N Richmond, 0.0630578305293 miles. Its category is Neighborhood Greenway; the label alone does not prove a project name, motor-vehicle access, or whether it is a connector. Include it in the catalog queue and resolve geometry and movement with local evidence.
- Connector classification and named-route membership cannot be inferred from source street names alone. Segment geometries and cross streets are review evidence, while project plans and field inspection establish their actual role.

The City's [Bike_Routes_EB ArcGIS layer](https://services7.arcgis.com/A03QrhyHnDaUmK0W/ArcGIS/rest/services/Bike_Routes_EB/FeatureServer/0?f=pjson) has similar fields and defaults `Source` to Chicago Data Portal. Its data last edit is 2024-03-20 (UTC) and the [item](https://www.arcgis.com/sharing/rest/content/items/c68368fa7786458c86f7e7006ff82ee3?f=pjson) was modified in November 2024; it is not evidence of a newer inventory than the December 2025 Socrata metadata. Search and this layer review did not establish another authoritative, current citywide installed-only greenway inventory, nor a source mapping all rows to named projects. Planned project maps include projects at different stages and cannot be merged as installed routes without status checks. Ask CDOT for current inventory and definitions if an up-to-date installed-only census is required.

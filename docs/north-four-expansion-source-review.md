# Ravenswood, Greenview, Wellington and Ridgeway source review

Reviewed October 27, 2026. [Structured observations](../data/routes/review-evidence.json) cover all 14 exact Neighborhood Greenway city rows. The city snapshot is a designation inventory through December 2025, not installation or current access; the OSM extract is dated September 11, 2026. All movement conclusions remain source-inferred. Source IDs, candidate ways, exact searches, retrieval failures and source metadata are in the JSON.

Ravenswood's School–Roscoe city row is separate from Wilson–Peterson. The latter lies on the east side of the railway. The 47th Ward chief of staff described Wilson–Leland as two-way, and Lawrence–Winnemac as northbound with a southbound bicycle lane; these statements and OSM tags do not authorize southbound cars on the northbound section. The boundary at Ainslie is node 9165694309 within way 117007075, and the Ainslie–Winnemac node slice ends at 257172686. Do not select an opposite-side street merely because it shares the Ravenswood name.

A [47th Ward graphic headed “CDOT Planned Street Redesign”](https://chi.streetsblog.org/wp-content/uploads/sites/15/2024/05/Screen-Shot-2024-05-03-at-4.55.57-PM.png), visually inspected, depicts the east leg around Lawrence and Ainslie with a northbound motor arrow, southbound contraflow bicycle lane, raised crossings, curb extensions, a speed hump and a protected section north of Ainslie. It is a **2024 proposal**, not an as-built. The [47th Ward staff account](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2024/05/03/which-metra-corridor-would-become-more-bike-friendly-and-greener-under-a-new-plan-ravenswood) describes its scope and an independent 2026 report describes a ribbon cutting; neither verifies each current sign or turn. The [40th Ward's Q1 2024 town hall PDF](https://40thward.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/03/40th-Ward-Q1-2024-Town-Hall-Presentation-1.pdf), PDF page 24, was visually inspected: the slide “2024 Major Developments” lists Ravenswood arterial resurfacing without segment limits or motor layout. Its browser fetch was too large; a public download succeeded.

Greenview Farwell–Jonquil is a single city row with no city motor-direction assertion; OSM 624602064 is northbound at Farwell. CDOT and the 49th Ward's 2016 project proposal was reported by [Active Transportation Alliance](https://activetransportation.org/blog/biking-from-edgewater-to-evanston-to-become-safer/) and [DNAinfo](https://www.dnainfo.com/chicago/20160115/rogers-park/can-evanston-to-edgewater-bike-path-plan-calm-commutes-for-cyclists/): the bike route continues north to Jonquil, with a southbound bicycle exception on the first block. The project proposal does not establish current signs. Four gaps in ordinary named street geometry at Greenview's mapped circles are bridged by circular one-way residential ways 1535604732, 1535604735, 1410844440 and 1535604025, with exact entry/exit nodes in JSON.

At Greenview/Ridge, two opposite one-way OSM arms share terminal nodes 12233741042 and 735250172. The northbound way 1322012667 is 55.61 m; southbound way 1322012663 is 56.41 m. At 1 m sampling intervals they remain within 2.55 m and 4.25 m respectively of the city line. The named arms contain six road-branch nodes, whose exact node/incident-way inventories, stop/crossing tags and exhaustive staged restriction-relation scan are in observation `greenview-ridge-opposing-arms`. Those branches do not themselves create an off-route bypass; evaluate restrictions and the actual direction-specific arm. The local scan found no matching restriction relations, not confirmation of present signs.

Wellington's Kimball–Sacramento Avondale rows are distinct from the 2025–26 Lakeview Wellington/Oakdale project. The city says westbound, as do the corresponding staged OSM ways. The [2023 project account](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2023/07/27/cdot-just-installed-a-neighborhood-greenway-on-wellington-in-avondale-why-did-it-happen-6-years-after-the-alder-asked-for-it) reports installation and the alderman's 2017 request, but an associated pedestrian island at Kimball should not be conflated with a motor restriction. Albany node 261145209 splits OSM way 24076328 at the city row boundary. Bicycle contraflow is not permission for cars.

Ridgeway has three distant components: 19th–Independence, Bloomingdale–Cortland, and Argyle–Carmen. North Lawndale way 24068883 has no one-way tag while the city asserts northbound; reverse car access is therefore conflicting/unknown. At Bloomingdale, the city terminus lies about 26 m south of OSM turning-circle node 261118560. That node joins the mapped cycleway 1418787325 and motor road 24071229; do not manufacture a motor connector to the city endpoint. On Argyle–Carmen the city direction is blank. A [City Council committee summary](https://occprodstoragev1.blob.core.usgovcloudapi.net/meetingattachmentspublic/218e4fd6-b343-4672-b6c1-c5e1abacb1e1.pdf), printed and PDF page 7, visually inspected, lists 20 mph on Ridgeway from Argyle to the North Branch Trail. A speed limit is not a motor filter, and committee action alone does not verify installation.

The exact searches and failed retrievals are recorded in JSON. No detailed block-level engineering drawings or direct official alderman construction updates were located for Greenview, Avondale Wellington, the southern and central Ridgeway sections, or Ravenswood School–Roscoe. The source file retains the contemporaneous project accounts and the applicable 47th Ward drawing. Absence from these searches is not proof that further plans do not exist.

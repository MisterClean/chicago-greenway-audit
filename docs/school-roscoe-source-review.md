# School and Lakeview Roscoe source review

Reviewed October 27, 2026. Evidence applies to Lakeview School and Roscoe only; the separate Northwest Side Roscoe greenway has different city source rows and is outside this review. The 44th Ward design begins at Lincoln, so it must not be projected west to Ravenswood. `data/routes/review-evidence.json` binds each record to explicit city row IDs, without assigning unverified OSM way IDs or approving movements.

The December 2025 city Bike Routes snapshot labels School Ravenswood–Clark eastbound and Lakeview Roscoe Ravenswood–Lake Shore Drive westbound. These are source attributes, not current sign inspections, and do not prove all intersection movements. The 2017 CDOT presentation describes the couplet and a typical one-way cross section; westbound and eastbound bike routes use different streets, not blanket contraflow rights for either motor direction.

The [44th Ward project page](https://www.44thward.org/infrastructure/bike-pedestrian-infrastructure/school-aldine-roscoe-greenway/) states on August 20, 2018 that curb extensions, speed humps and most striping were installed; the School/Clifton raised crosswalk was still being installed, with an expected August 31 completion. A [firsthand report dated October 9](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2018/10/09/eyes-on-the-street-the-roscoe-and-schoolaldine-greenways-are-ready-to-ride) describes the crossing and the Roscoe/Broadway raised bike connector as built. These are historical observations, not proof of present signs or access. Raised crossings, humps and ordinary curb extensions are traffic calming and cannot automatically be counted as through-car barriers.

The [east long plot](https://www.44thward.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/LongPlot_-_School-Aldine__Roscoe_Clark_to_LSD.pdf), page 1, depicts the offset Roscoe/Broadway junction, with a raised contraflow bike connector on Broadway. A cyclist's path is not evidence of a permissible car path; separately trace the motor approach, Broadway turns, departure and designation correspondence. The drawing also shows bike crossings and a full-block curb extension near Inner Lake Shore Drive. Do not infer car access through the lakefront underpass. The [west long plot](https://www.44thward.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/08/LongPlot_-_School-Aldine__Roscoe_Lincoln_to_C.pdf), page 1, shows the School curb extensions, speed humps and Clifton raised crossing. Both are designs, not as-built certifications; a project completion label does not settle each movement.

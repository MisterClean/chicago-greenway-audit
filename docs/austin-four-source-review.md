# Austin four street families: source review

Reviewed October 27, 2026. The [structured observations](../data/routes/review-evidence.json) cover all 13 city rows with exact `displayrou = Neighborhood Greenway`: two Hirsch, three Le Moyne, six Lavergne and two Menard. City data records designation through December 2025, not installation or present motor access. The cited OSM data is the September 11, 2026 snapshot. Candidate ways and direction interpretations need exact node clipping, junction/restriction checks and field verification; none is field approved.

Hirsch (Austin–Lavergne) is eastbound in the city inventory and in the mapped road segments. Between Leclaire and Leamington, OSM way 1230671144 is a bicycle/foot-designated cycleway rather than a motor road. Its relationship to the exact city line and any legal motor movement needs inspection; do not bridge it with a car run or detour. Le Moyne runs parallel, westbound from Austin through part of the Linder–Lavergne city row. In particular OSM way 583154158 remains westbound east of Linder, notwithstanding that row's city `oneway_dir = -`. Other eastern ways have no one-way tag, which supports only an OSM inference, not a verified current sign. The city Linder endpoint falls inside an OSM way and requires an exact slice.

Lavergne has opposing motor directions: southbound Harrison–Fulton, northbound Kinzie–Chicago, and southbound Chicago–Thomas on the corresponding tagged OSM segments. Contraflow bicycle tags do not permit cars to travel against them. The city endpoint at Madison on the southern segment is about 63 m east of the northern city endpoint: neither row includes a connecting Madison segment, so do not join them by an assumed off-route route. At Kinzie the local snapshot maps parallel, oppositely directed ways 1213420157 and 1213420158; inspect each arm's exact attachment. The intervening Fulton–Kinzie and northern Thomas–Hirsch rows carry no city motor-direction assertion when marked `-`.

Menard Jackson–Washington is mapped largely two-way; Washington–Lake includes northbound OSM way 236759650. Parallel opposing ways 582888784 and 582888785 at Lake need independent attachment and directional review. The city row ends at Lake; way 59148878 extends north beyond the city endpoint and is not assigned to the row. These observations do not establish junction legality or current conditions.

## Search limits and exclusions

The JSON retains all exact searches. Those searches did not locate detailed corridor-specific CDOT engineering sheets or actual alderman project updates for these four Austin scopes. This is a documented search gap, not a claim that no such documents exist. The broad planning maps described below were visually inspected, but provide no street-level motor restriction evidence. A March 2026 [Portage Park network proposal](https://chi.streetsblog.org/2026/03/06/bikeway-hearing-parking-lot-talking-with-folks-from-the-massive-crowd-outside-and-inside-the-portage-park-neighborhood-bike-network-meeting) mentions Menard between Berteau and Lawrence; it is a different geography and proposed, so supplies no evidence for Menard Jackson–Lake. Search hits for Oak Park and Melrose Park Le Moyne/Hirsch describe different municipalities and are excluded. The citywide bike maps are not detailed engineering plans and were not used to infer motor movement.

The source JSON and SQLite extract were inspected read-only. IDs in the observation file are candidates, not a verified complete geometry trace; in particular terminal portions and parallel ways require topology review. No proposed or ordinary bike facility outside the exact city category has been added.

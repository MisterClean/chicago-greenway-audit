# Northwest Roscoe source review

Reviewed October 27, 2026. This covers Narragansett–Kedvale, separate from Lakeview Roscoe. The city source contains four records, with eastbound motor direction and bicycle contraflow on parts of the corridor; the Cicero–Kilbourn record has no cardinal motor direction. Bicycle two-way attributes are not motor permissions.

The CDOT-authored network diagram in [Bikes for Belmont Cragin, Figure 1.3](https://sharedusemobilitycenter.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/BikesForBelmontCragin_v5.pdf#page=9), printed p.7/PDF p.9, was visually inspected. Roscoe is a red planned-bike-route line. The map provides corridor context but no motor arrows, intersection design, diverters or turn prohibitions. Its 2020 label is the plan date; the enclosing report is dated August 2023. The older EnglishVersion URL returned 404, so the currently hosted v5 copy was used.

The [July 21, 2021 council referral report](https://chicityclerk.s3.amazonaws.com/s3fs-public/document_uploads/council-reports/2021/Referred%20Legislation%20%28Redacted%29%2C%202021.07.21%20-%20Section%203a%20-%20Aldermanic%20Introductions%20Traffic%20Regulations.pdf#page=5), printed/PDF pp.5–6, lists Reboyras, Cardona and Villegas proposals to amend specified eastbound one-way segments. Page 6 was visually checked. These are introductions referred to committee, not proof of adoption or physical installation. Speed-limit proposals do not interrupt continuous access. PDF hashes are saved in the structured evidence.

[CDOT's August 2021 newsletter](https://myemail.constantcontact.com/Chicago-Department-of-Transportation-Newsletter-August-2021.html?aid=ara2MktjJgU&soid=1133951929146) documents collaboration with the three wards on neighborhood bicycle infrastructure, but supplies no Roscoe-specific movement rules. Searches of ward project/newsletter sources and visits to the 30th and 36th Ward sites did not locate original Roscoe construction notices or detailed drawings. The 31st Ward host could not be retrieved. These are gaps in this review, not claims that records do not exist.

The app therefore retains snapshot inference and flags conflicting circulation at Keeler. The 44th Ward plans must not appear as support for this Northwest Side section. Obtain intersection/striping drawings, relevant completion records and dated signage observations before treating the route as fully reviewed.
